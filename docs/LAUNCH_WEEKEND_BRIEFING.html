What's Changed (Pricing & Billing)

A quick plain-English rundown of the pricing and billing changes now live in the system.

  • Multi-day jobs now get cheaper per day, automatically. We've moved away from the old "work out a percentage discount" approach. Now each day on a job is simply priced a bit lower than the day before — Day 2 costs less than Day 1, Day 3 less again, down to a floor rate. The multi-day saving is built straight into the rates, so there's no separate discount to calculate or apply by hand. Longer jobs come out cheaper per day without anyone doing the maths.
  • The AFD is now the "HEPA Air Scrubber" — and it's now billed. Same piece of equipment, clearer name. It's charged at $100 per unit, per day. Previously we recorded that it was used but never actually put it on the bill, so that usage was going out for free. Now it's captured on the job completion form and flows through onto the invoice.
  • Waste disposal is now charged by volume. It used to be noted down but never billed. Now it's priced by how much is removed (in cubic metres), on a set price schedule, and it appears on the customer's bill.
  • Fixed a data-safety bug on job completion. Before, if a save failed at the wrong moment (say the connection dropped), a job could get marked "submitted" with incomplete details behind it. That can't happen quietly anymore — if the save doesn't go through, the system stops and tells the technician to try again, so nothing gets sent based on half-saved information.
