Last reviewed: 29 July 2026

1. What changed at launch

Two things we used to do for free are now billed.

  • The AFD is now the "HEPA Air Scrubber" — and it's now billed. Same piece of equipment, clearer name, $100 per unit per day. We used to record that it was used but never put it on the bill. Units and days are captured on the job completion form and flow onto the invoice.
  • Waste disposal is now billed. It used to be noted down as a rough size and never charged. Now it's priced by volume removed (Section 4) and appears on the invoice.

Also: multi-day jobs now get cheaper per day automatically. The old "work out a percentage discount" approach is gone — the saving is built straight into the day rates instead.

2. Labour rates

Day 1 is the full 8-hour rate. Each later day is charged at its own lower rate, down to a floor from Day 5. That decline is the multi-day discount — there's nothing to calculate or apply by hand.

Under 2 hours — flat 2-hour minimum, no cheaper partial rate. 2 to 8 hours — scales smoothly between the 2-hour minimum and the Day 1 rate. Beyond 8 hours — whole days at the declining rates, with any leftover hours charged as a fraction of the next day's rate.

Example: 3 full days of normal work = $1,245.33 + $1,060.34 + $1,054.52 = $3,360.19, before equipment and GST.

4. Waste disposal

Priced by volume removed. Never discounted. On site you enter the volume, the system works out the price, and you have to confirm it — or type a different figure if the real cost was different.

In-between volumes scale smoothly (1 m³ is $175, 3 m³ is $400, 7 m³ is $626.50). Above 12 m³ adds $145 per extra m³.

5. Discount cap and GST

The 13% cap. There's no automatic discount any more — the multi-day saving lives in the day rates. 13% is now only the ceiling on a manual discount an admin can choose to apply at invoice time, and it comes off labour only — never equipment or waste.

GST is 10%, worked out on the whole subtotal and added last.

6. Worth knowing

The three gaps previously listed here are now closed (late-July update). What changed:

  • HEPA can now be estimated at the inspection — and it shows on the customer's quote. When scrubbers are planned, the units and days go in on the inspection form, the cost lands in the quoted price, and a HEPA line appears on the customer's report. Days can be left on auto, where they simply follow the job's equipment days.
  • Waste disposal now shows on the customer's quote. The report lists the waste line alongside the equipment costs. On a two-option quote it appears once, marked "billed once" — it applies whichever option goes ahead, and it deliberately stays out of the two option totals.
  • Actual waste volume is captured at job completion. The technician records what was actually removed, the system re-prices it, and the technician confirms (or overrides) it on site. On the invoice screen the estimate and the actual sit side by side — the admin taps the one to bill. Nothing auto-overwrites the other.
  • The job report now shows the equipment and waste actually used — each item with days and cost, matching what the invoice will say.
